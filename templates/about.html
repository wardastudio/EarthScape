About EarthScape

EarthScape is a climate intelligence platform built to monitor, analyze, and predict climate patterns using big data and machine learning.

Our Mission

To empower climate agencies, researchers, and policymakers with actionable insights derived from satellite, weather station, and environmental sensor data.

Data Sources
  • Satellite Imagery (Sentinel, Landsat, MODIS)
  • Weather Station Networks (NOAA, ECMWF)
  • Environmental Sensors (AQI, CO₂, Temperature)
  • Oceanographic Buoys (Marine Salinity, Sea Level)
Technology Stack
Python Flask MongoDB SQLite Hadoop/HDFS Machine Learning Chart.js Leaflet
