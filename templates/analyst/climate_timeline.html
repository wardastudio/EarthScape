Chronology of Earth System Changes

Scrollable history from pre-industrial markers to projected future climate thresholds.

Pre-Industrial Baseline (1880)

Atmospheric CO2 concentrations balanced at approximately 280 ppm. Average temperature anomaly defined as 0.00°C.

Satellite Observation Era Begins (1979)

Space agency radar programs begin tracking polar ice sheet elevation profiles and ocean surface temperatures continuously.

The Paris Climate Agreement (2015)

International treaty targets global average warming limits below +1.5°C to avert critical ecosystem collapses.

Projected Climate Tipping Horizons (2050)

Under unchecked RCP 8.5 scenarios, global ocean warmth anomalies are modeled to exceed +2.6°C, initiating rapid arctic sheet melting cycles.
