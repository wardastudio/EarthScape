Global Climate Indicators

Observed changes in Earth's energy balance and key environmental systems.

Ocean Heat Content Anomaly

The oceans absorb over 90% of the excess heat trapped in the atmosphere. Current ocean heat content has reached a record high of 345 Zettajoules above the 1981–2010 average.

Record Anomaly
Sea Level Rise Acceleration

Global mean sea level has risen by approximately 24 centimeters since 1880. Satellite telemetry indicates an acceleration in rise rates to 4.4 mm per year.

Rising +4.4mm/yr
Glacial Volume Contraction

Glaciers monitored by the World Glacier Monitoring Service have lost mass for 36 consecutive years. Polar ice caps show unprecedented melt volumes.

Mass Loss Active
Atmospheric Moisture Telemetry

Higher surface temperatures lead to increased evaporation rates, resulting in tropospheric column moisture levels rising at a rate of 7% per degree Celsius.

Humidity Spike
