Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to operational capabilities, sensor ingestion cycles, and research permission schemas.

How often is the satellite imagery telemetry updated?
Satellite telemetry is updated every 6 hours via the Sentinel-2 and Landsat-9 downlink pipelines. Real‑time data is available for selected regions.
Can I download raw NetCDF or GeoTIFF datasets?
Yes, approved researchers and analysts can download raw datasets in NetCDF, GeoTIFF, and HDF5 formats via the Dataset Management portal.
What machine learning models do you use for climate predictions?
We currently use Linear Regression for carbon predictions, Decision Trees for flood risk, and KNN for heatwave detection. Ensemble models are in development.
Is there a developer API available?
Yes! Our REST API is available for authenticated users. Contact support for API keys and documentation.
How can I get access to historical climate data?
Historical data from 1880 to the present is available through the Historical Analysis dashboard for analysts and researchers.
